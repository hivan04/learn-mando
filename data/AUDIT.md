## D. Grammar notes worth flagging

- **辣 (là)** — your line "不难吃太辣的东西" should be **不可以吃太辣的东西** (bù kěyǐ chī tài là de). 难吃 nánchī means "tastes bad".
- **需要帮忙收完吗** → **收碗** (shōu wǎn, clear the bowls). 完 wán means "finish", 碗 wǎn is "bowl".
- **Days of the week** — your note lists 一，二，三，四，**无**，六，天. Should be **五** (wǔ). 无 wú means "none".
- **我这星我 = this week i...** → **我这星期** (wǒ zhè xīngqī).
- **两杯冰水** — 冰水 bīngshuǐ is iced water, not "cold water" (冷水 lěngshuǐ).
